# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Send a private message to the agency founder at ttrompier@newgenesis.ai with:

- what you found and where (URL, repository or file);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- the impact you expect.

We acknowledge a report within three working days and tell you when it is fixed.

## What we protect

- Client sites and their data, which we treat as confidential.
- Accounts and secrets: they are never stored in a repository.
- The personal data of our team, who are young people: data minimisation applies everywhere.

## Our baseline

- Two-factor authentication for every member of the organisation.
- Least privilege: read access by default, write access per team and per repository.
- Weekly grouped dependency updates and security alerts on every repository.
- No secret in code, logs, commits or documentation.
