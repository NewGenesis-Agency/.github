# NewGenesis

NewGenesis is an agency of websites and branding for craftsmen, shops and local businesses.

We design the identity (logo, colours, typefaces, charter) and the website: a clear, fast showcase, or an
immersive experience with motion design and 3D. Every site is simple to edit yourself and prepared to be read and
understood by Google and AI assistants.

- Website: https://newgenesis.ai
- Contact: contact@newgenesis.ai
- Our repositories are private. Each client site lives in its own repository, named `client-<name>`.
